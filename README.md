This dataset contains raw neurophysiological and pain-outcome data from 42 human participants who took part in up to three experimental sessions. Across the sessions, the study compared three transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) conditions: Active (2 mA), Minimal intensity (0.3 mA), and Low intensity (0.0 mA). Data are organised by participant (`sub-XX`) and session (`ses-01`, `ses-02`, and `ses-03`). Within each session, resting-state EEG recordings are labelled `task-rest` and TMS-evoked EEG recordings are labelled `task-tep`. The acquisition labels `acq-01pre` and `acq-02post` identify recordings collected before and after the session’s stimulation procedure. TEP refers to a TMS-evoked potential, which is the EEG response measured following a TMS pulse. The accompanying pain-outcome data include pain ratings, heat pain threshold (HPT), and pressure pain threshold (PPT), with `post-0` and `post-30` indicating measurements collected immediately and 30 minutes after stimulation, respectively.

The EEG and TMS-EEG recordings are stored in BrainVision format as linked `.vhdr`, `.vmrk`, and `.eeg` files. The `.vhdr` file should be opened when loading a recording, the `.eeg` file contains the recorded EEG signal, and the `.vmrk` file contains the associated event and stimulus markers. Original motor-evoked potential files named `MEPpre`, `MEPpost5`, and `MEPpost25` are retained as text files under `sourcedata`; these are source MEP recordings and have not been converted into validated EMG-BIDS files. All EEG and TMS-EEG recordings are raw and have not been preprocessed, so users should apply and document appropriate filtering, re-referencing, artefact removal, and TMS-artefact correction before analysis.
